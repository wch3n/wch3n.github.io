Recent work · MACE-FNO, 2026

An efficient, configuration-dependent kernel for long-range physics in machine-learning potentials

Most machine-learning interatomic potentials only see atoms within a finite cutoff. Long-range extensions couple learned sources across the cell through a kernel, but that kernel is usually fixed, either prescribed as Coulomb or learned once for all configurations.

MACE-FNO, developed with Gian-Marco Rignanese, learns the kernel with a Fourier neural operator on top of a local MACE model, from energies and forces alone. Its nonlinear form adapts the coupling between distant ions to the surrounding medium, which no fixed kernel can do once the screening varies in space. In the bulk benchmarks, this comes at a cost comparable to that of a prescribed-kernel model.
